Main navigation:
Home -> Category page -> Individual product page

The cart uses localStorage in the browser. Checkout shows PayPal and Credit / Debit Card as payment preferences and continues the order conversation through WhatsApp. The site does not collect card numbers.

To add a new product, edit product.js and add a product object to window.PRODUCTS. Use a unique id and the exact image path.
For new Watch or Accessories products, use category: "Watches" or category: "Accessories".

Note: the supplied catalog currently contains Bags and Shoes data. Watches and Accessories pages are ready for future products.
