FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Clear information before you place an order.

How do I order a product?

Open the product page, choose Add to Cart or Order on WhatsApp. For a cart order, open the shopping bag and continue to checkout, then choose the payment preference you want to discuss with us.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. The store currently advertises free worldwide shipping with an estimated delivery window of 9–13 working days.

Which payment methods can I use?

PayPal and card payment can be discussed during checkout. The website does not collect or store card details; payment instructions are confirmed through the order conversation.

Can I ask about a product before ordering?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on any product page and the message will include the product name, ID and listed price.
